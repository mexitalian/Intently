<p><b>Are you using your phone, or is your phone using you?</b></p>
<p><i>Intently</i> is a minimal, ad-free Android launcher built around intentions. Give each home-screen row a word — Read, Listen, Create, Move — and keep only the apps that serve it.</p>
<p><br><b>FEATURES YOU MIGHT LIKE:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Intention-first home screen:</b> Name each row (Read, Listen, Create, Move…), choose the app that opens on tap, and swipe up to open its folder view. A single member behaves like a plain app; more members make a folder.</li>
<li><b>App rename labels:</b> Give any app a bespoke name shown on the home screen and in the drawer.</li>
<li><b>Fast app drawer:</b> Search by name and launch automatically when one result matches.</li>
<li><b>Useful gestures:</b> Double tap to lock, swipe up for all apps, swipe up on an intention for its folder, and optional left/right shortcuts.</li>
<li><b>Home app slots:</b> Show 1–8 apps directly on the home screen.</li>
<li><b>Screen time:</b> Today's screen time at a glance, with a configurable app to open for details.</li>
<li><b>Customizations:</b> Resize text, bold font, rename apps, hide unused apps, adjust alignment, show or hide the status bar, and choose date/time display.</li>
<li><b>Wallpaper:</b> A new wallpaper every day, or use any image from your gallery.</li>
<li><b>Privacy:</b> No data collection, no ads, no trackers. Free and open source under GPLv3.</li>
<li><b>Modern Android:</b> Dark and light themes, work profile support, and Private Space support on supported devices.</li>
<li><b>About page:</b> FAQs, tips, and the complete list of hidden niche features.</li>
</ul>
<p>To keep the home screen simple, a few niche features are available but hidden. Please visit the <b>About</b> page in settings for the complete list.</p>
<p><b>FAQs:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>1. Hidden apps</b> - Long press anywhere on the home screen to open settings. Tap <b>Intently</b> at the top to see your hidden apps.</li>
<li><b>2. Navigation gestures</b> - Some devices do not support gestures with downloaded Android launchers. This can only be fixed by your device manufacturer via an update.</li>
<li><b>3. Wallpapers</b> - Intently provides a new wallpaper daily. You can also set any wallpaper from your phone settings or Gallery/Photos app.</li>
</ul>
<p>The About page in settings has the rest of the FAQs and several tips to help you get the most out of Intently.</p>
<p><b>Accessibility Service:</b> The Accessibility Service is used exclusively to let you turn off the phone screen with a double-tap gesture. It is optional, disabled by default, and does not collect or share any data.</p>
